What Do Women Really Want?

The Lancelot Answer: Sovereignty. No buts.

One of the best and most timeless answers comes from Emre Gover (@emregover) on X:

Women want sovereignty over their own lives. Not conditional permission. Not control with better branding. No “but.”

There is an old story about Sir Lancelot. He is asked the impossible question: What do women really want?

He searches everywhere. Knights, nobles, wise men, and ordinary people all give different answers: love, beauty, loyalty, wealth, romance, security, status, power.

But the real answer comes from an old witch:

A woman wants sovereignty over her own life.

No buts

The important part is not only the word sovereignty. The important part is that sovereignty cannot come with a hidden condition.

Not “I respect her, but…”
Not “She is free, but…”
Not “She can choose, but…”
Not “I support her dreams, but…”

The moment you add a “but,” it is no longer sovereignty. It is conditional permission.

The timeless answer

So what do women really want?

They want what most human beings want: dignity, safety, respect, desire without reduction, support without control, and the freedom to be the author of their own life.

Loved without being owned. Supported without being controlled. Respected without conditions.
