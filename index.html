What Does a Woman Really Want?

The Lancelot answer: sovereignty. No buts. Except the final but.

One of the best and most timeless answers comes from Emre Gover (@emregover) on X:

A woman wants sovereignty over her own life — to choose for herself. No buts.

The story

There is an old story about Lancelot and a witch.

Lancelot is asked the impossible question: What does a woman really want?

People give him all kinds of answers: beauty, love, wealth, loyalty, romance, security, power, and admiration.

But the witch gives the real answer:

A woman wants sovereignty — the right to choose for herself.

Not permission. Not protection with conditions. Not “I respect you, but…” Not “You are free, as long as…” Not “I support you, if…”

Sovereignty. No buts.

The twist

Later, Lancelot is given a choice about the witch’s fate.

Should she be beautiful by day and ugly by night, or ugly by day and beautiful by night?

For once, he does not decide for her. He understands the answer and says:

You choose.

The witch is delighted. Because he gives her real sovereignty, the spell breaks — and she chooses to remain beautiful forever.

The meaning

The answer was never “give her what you think is best.”

The answer was: let her own her choice.

So the clean answer is this:

A woman wants to be loved without being owned, supported without being controlled, desired without being reduced, and respected without conditions.

No buts.

The final but

But there is one final “but”: even the most beautiful woman in the world may reveal the witch inside her if she never gets what she wants.
